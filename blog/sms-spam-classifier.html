SMS Spam Classifier

An NLP model that tells spam from ham at 97.2% precision.

Problem

Spam messages clutter inboxes and put users at risk.

Solution

An NLP classifier using Multinomial Naive Bayes that labels SMS as spam or ham, reaching 97.2% precision after tuning and model selection.
